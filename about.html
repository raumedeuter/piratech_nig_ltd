Who we are

A Nigerian technology company helping businesses transform

Pira-Tech Nigeria Limited is a privately owned technology solutions company headquartered in Abuja. We deliver bespoke digital systems, consultancy services, and operational support to medium and large organizations.

Our strength is practical execution. We combine clear thinking, technical depth, and collaborative delivery to solve real business problems.

  • Business-aligned ICT strategy
  • Reliable implementation and support
